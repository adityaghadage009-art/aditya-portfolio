Brand IDENTITY

Girvan At Girvan, guests are offered the staycation of their dreams, where premium luxury merges seamlessly with nostalgic charm. Nestled in the heart of Gir National Park, this resort beautifully captures the essence of old India, allowing visitors to experience the elegance of a vintage Indian village. Here, the timeless beauty of by gone eras comes to life, inviting everyone to savour a unique blend of tradition and tranquillity. Girvan sought a minimalist, modern logo that reflected its blend of luxury and nostalgia. Inspired by the simplicity of the Nike logo, the design was sleek and contemporary, avoiding typical animal motifs. It subtly embodied the timeless charm of old India, offering a refined and elegant representation of the resort’s unique character.
