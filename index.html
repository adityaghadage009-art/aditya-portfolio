My 9 to 5s

The Thinking Behind the Pixels

Attention to details

I’m the kind of designer who notices when a button is two pixels off. Six years in, that habit has only got stronger.

I studied design for four years, and I’ve spent the last two-plus doing it for real, for banks, startups and brands that had nothing in common. College gave me the basics. Real users are where I actually learned.

Learning by doing
Empathy

Most of what I know came from watching people use things. The customer who gives up on a form halfway. The person who calls support because one label wasn’t clear. Those moments tell you more than any brief. So I listen, I watch, and I keep asking why until the real problem shows up.

Curiosity
Designing for delight

Getting it to work is step one. But I want people to enjoy using it too. A friendly line of copy, a small animation, an illustration that makes someone smile. That’s the stuff people remember.

Which is probably why I still draw. Branding and illustration aren’t a side thing for me. They’re how I give a product its own personality.

Lately, AI has become a big part of how I work. I use it to try more ideas, build prototypes quicker and test with users earlier. It saves me time. Deciding what’s actually good is still my job.
