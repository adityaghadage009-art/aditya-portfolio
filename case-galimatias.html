El Galimatias El Galimatías is not just a creative agency - it’s a curious companion for brands navigating the chaos

of ideas. We believe that brilliance rarely arrives fully formed. It’s born in the mess, the margin notes, the moments that make no sense at first glance. That’s where we come in. We take the scribbles, the what-ifs, the weird ideas that don’t yet have names - and turn them into something that makes people feel.

We’re not afraid of strange. We welcome it. Because in our world, nonsense isn’t the opposite of clarity - it’s the path to it.

"You bring the spark. We’ll help it speak."

TYPOGRAPHY

The typography system of El Galimatías balances clarity and character. Gilroy, our primary font, brings boldness and modern structure - it’s confident, clean, and perfect for big, impactful moments. Satoshi, the secondary font, offers calm contrast with its minimalist design, allowing space for ideas to breathe without distraction.
