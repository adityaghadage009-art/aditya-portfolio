We dove deep into the world of design tools, conducting desk research and exploring how designers interact with them. From endless clicks to hidden features, we left no stone unturned in our creative journey. Our mission? To uncover the gaps-the moments where tools feel clunky, creativity slows, or users feel stuck. By understanding these pain points, we crafted a UI that doesn’t just work-it works for you.

Benchmarking allows us to analyze the strengths and weaknesses of existing tools and systems, such as Material Design and Untitled UI. It helps identify best practices and uncover opportunities to develop a tool that truly caters to designers’ needs.
